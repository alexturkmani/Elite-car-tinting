VLT (visible light transmission) is measured through the glass and film together. NSW passenger cars: front side windows 35% or lighter, rear side and rear windows 20% or lighter. Goods vehicles and some other vehicles have different rules. Screens vary, so we'll show you real film samples on your car before we start.
