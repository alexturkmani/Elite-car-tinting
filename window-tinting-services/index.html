Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How dark can I legally go in NSW?

For passenger cars, front side windows must be 35% VLT or lighter and rear side and rear windows 20% or lighter. The windscreen can only have a tinted band across the top. Goods vehicles have different rear-window rules, so ask us about vans and utes.

How long does a car take?

Most sedans, hatches and SUVs are finished in two to four hours. Old tint removal adds time, and we'll tell you exactly how long when you book.

When can I wind my windows down?

Leave windows up for three to five days while the adhesive cures. A slight haze or small water pockets during curing are normal and clear on their own.

Will tint affect my phone, GPS or toll tag?

Our carbon and ceramic films contain no metal, so they don't interfere with phone signal, GPS, keyless entry or e-tags.

What does the warranty cover?

Our lifetime workmanship warranty covers peeling, bubbling, cracking and fading for as long as you own the vehicle or property.
