Our film

Why we use 3M

3M's car films contain no metal, so they don't affect your phone, GPS or toll tag. They hold their colour and come with 3M's limited lifetime warranty.

We carry three 3M ranges. Color Stable gives a neutral black look. Ceramic IR adds more heat rejection. Crystalline rejects the most heat, even in very light shades.
