Common questions

Suitable tint levels depend on your vehicle, existing glass and local requirements. Your installer can confirm the options for each window.

Installation time depends on your vehicle and whether old film needs to be removed. Ask for a time estimate when booking.

Follow the care instructions provided by your installer. The curing time depends on the film and weather conditions.

Carbon and ceramic films generally avoid the interference associated with metallic films. Ask about the construction of the film you choose.

Carbon tint uses carbon particles to block heat and UV rays, it's a great mid-range option with no signal interference and a lifetime warranty. Ceramic tint uses nano-ceramic particles for the highest performance: up to 80% heat rejection, 99% UV blocking, crystal-clear visibility, and a lifetime warranty. Ceramic is the premium choice for maximum comfort and longevity.

Warranty terms depend on the selected film and manufacturer. Confirm coverage and care requirements before installation.
